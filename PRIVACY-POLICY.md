# Privacy Policy — CUCO Currency Converter

**Last updated: 28 October 2026**

CUCO Currency Converter ("CUCO", "the app") is developed and published by
**mochitg45**. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with
your information.

## The short version

CUCO has no accounts and no server of its own. Everything you create in the
app — your saved currency list, rate alerts, trips and expenses, and your
settings — is stored **only on your device**. We cannot see it, and it is never
uploaded to us.

The app does two things that involve the network: it downloads exchange rates,
and it shows advertisements. On Android it also sends usage
statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase.

## Information the app stores on your device

The following stays on your phone and is never transmitted to us:

- The currencies in your rates list, and their order
- Your rate alerts and whether they have triggered
- Your trips (name, destination, dates and budget), expenses, categories, notes,
  and the exchange rate recorded with each expense
- The ids of the calendar events an imported trip came from
- Your settings: home currency, chosen theme, and recently used currency pairs
- A cached copy of the most recent exchange rates, so the app works offline

Uninstalling CUCO deletes all of it. There is no server-side copy, so there is
nothing for us to delete on request and no account to close.

## Exchange rate data

To show current rates, the app requests publicly available data from:

- **open.er-api.com** — fiat currency rates
- **CoinGecko** (`api.coingecko.com`) — Bitcoin, gold and silver prices
- **Frankfurter** (`api.frankfurter.dev`) — historical rates for the charts

These are ordinary web requests. They do not carry your name, your device
identifiers, or anything you have entered in the app. As with any internet
request, the provider necessarily receives your device's IP address and can see
which currencies were requested. Each provider handles that under its own
privacy policy.

## Advertising

CUCO displays banner advertisements supplied by **Google AdMob**. To serve and
measure ads, Google's advertising SDK collects information from your device,
which typically includes your device's advertising identifier, IP address,
general device information, and your interactions with the ads shown.

This collection is performed by Google, not by us, and it is governed by
Google's own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from partner sites and apps:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

On Android you can limit this at any time in **Settings → Privacy → Ads**, where
you can reset or delete your advertising ID.

We do not receive your personal information from Google. We see only aggregate
reporting, such as how many ads were shown.

## In-app purchase

Remove ads is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play or the App Store. To check whether you own it, the app contacts RevenueCat, our purchase provider, each time it starts; RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase history for this app, and basic device and network information such as your IP address. RevenueCat does not receive your name or anything you enter in CUCO.

## Usage analytics (Android)

The Android version of CUCO uses **Google Analytics for Firebase** to count
how the app is used: which screens are opened (for example Today, Spend, Wallet or Tools), which features are used (such as completing a conversion, creating a
rate alert or adding an expense), the currency codes involved (for example USD
and EUR), the expense category chosen, how often the app is opened, and the
device model, operating system version, app version and approximate country.
It does not record amounts you type, trip names, dates, budgets, notes, calendar contents, or the values of your
expenses, wallet or alerts. This
data is collected by Google on our behalf and governed by Google's
[Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We see only aggregate
reports and use them to decide what to improve.

## Calendar

If you tap Find trips in my calendar, CUCO asks for permission to read your calendar. It looks for flight and hotel events from yesterday to twelve months ahead to suggest trips. Event titles, places and dates are read on your device only. When you add a suggested trip, CUCO saves that trip (its city, currency and dates) and the ids of the calendar events it came from, so they are not suggested again. Nothing else from your calendar is stored, and nothing is sent to us, to Google Analytics or to anyone else. You can turn calendar access off at any time in your device settings.

## Permissions

CUCO requests:

- **Internet access** — to download exchange rates and advertisements.
- **Notifications** — only if you create a rate alert and choose to allow them.
  Notifications are generated on your device; no notification content is sent
  to a server. You may decline, and the rest of the app works normally.
- **Calendar (read)** — only when you use Find trips in my calendar.

CUCO does **not** request location, contacts, camera, microphone, or access to
your files. It does not read or store any payment or banking details, and it
cannot move money.

## Children

CUCO is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information
from children.

## Accuracy of rates

Exchange rates in CUCO are provided for reference only. They come from free
public data, fiat rates update approximately once per day, and they will differ
from the rate a bank, card issuer or exchange actually gives you. Do not rely on
CUCO for financial, trading or investment decisions.
